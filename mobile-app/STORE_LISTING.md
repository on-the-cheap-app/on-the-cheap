## Full Description (4000 characters max)

### Main Description
Discover amazing restaurant deals, daily specials, and exclusive digital coupons in your area with On-the-Cheap! Save money while enjoying delicious meals at local restaurants, bars, and eateries.

**🍽️ FIND LOCAL DEALS**
Search thousands of restaurants near you offering special deals, happy hours, and limited-time promotions. Whether you're craving pizza, sushi, burgers, or fine dining, On-the-Cheap helps you find the best value.

**🎟️ DIGITAL COUPONS**
Access exclusive digital coupons from your favorite restaurants:
• Percentage discounts (10-50% off)
• Buy-One-Get-One (BOGO) deals
• Free appetizers and desserts
• Combo meal specials
• No paper coupons needed - show QR code to redeem

**📍 LOCATION-BASED SEARCH**
• Automatically finds deals near your current location
• Search by city, neighborhood, or address
• Filter by cuisine type, price range, and distance
• View deals on interactive map
• Get directions to restaurants

**💙 SAVE YOUR FAVORITES**
• Bookmark your favorite restaurants
• Save coupons for later use
• Get notified about new deals at saved venues
• Never miss a special at your go-to spots

**🔔 SMART NOTIFICATIONS**
• Real-time alerts for new deals nearby
• Expiring coupon reminders
• Special occasion offers
• Happy hour notifications
• Custom notification preferences

**🎯 KEY FEATURES**

✓ Browse daily specials by day of the week
✓ Happy hour finder with time restrictions
✓ Digital coupon wallet - no paper needed
✓ QR code redemption for instant savings
✓ Restaurant ratings and reviews
✓ Photos and detailed venue information
✓ Food truck and pop-up event tracking
✓ Share deals with friends and family
✓ Track your savings over time

**💰 HOW IT WORKS**

1. **Discover** - Browse restaurants and deals near you
2. **Save** - Add coupons to your digital wallet
3. **Redeem** - Show QR code at restaurant to save money
4. **Enjoy** - Eat great food for less!

**🌟 WHY ON-THE-CHEAP?**

• 100% Free to use - no subscription required
• Real verified deals from actual restaurants
• Updated daily with new specials
• Easy-to-use interface
• Privacy-focused - your data stays safe
• Support local businesses while saving money

**🍕 PERFECT FOR:**

• Budget-conscious diners
• Foodies exploring new restaurants
• Date night planning
• Happy hour enthusiasts
• Large group dining
• Students and families
• Anyone who loves great food at great prices
