## Full Description

### Google Play Store (4000 characters max):

Discover amazing restaurant deals and specials in your area with On the Cheap! Save money while enjoying great food at your favorite local restaurants.

🔥 KEY FEATURES:

• Find Daily Specials - Discover happy hours, lunch deals, and dinner specials near you
• Digital Coupons - Browse and redeem exclusive coupons from local restaurants
• Save Favorites - Keep track of your favorite deals for easy access
• Push Notifications - Get alerted when new specials are posted at your favorite spots
• Smart Filters - Search by cuisine type, price range, distance, and more
• Restaurant Info - View menus, hours, directions, and contact details
• QR Code Scanning - Quick coupon redemption with built-in scanner

💰 SAVE MONEY, EAT WELL:

Whether you're looking for lunch deals, happy hour specials, or weekend dining, On the Cheap helps you discover the best restaurant offers in your neighborhood. Never miss a great deal again!

From 2-for-1 appetizers to half-price entrees and complimentary desserts, find the perfect special for any occasion. Our platform makes it easy to explore new restaurants while keeping your budget in check.

📍 LOCAL & FRESH:

Support local restaurants while saving money. Our platform connects you directly with restaurant owners who offer exclusive deals and specials just for our users. Discover hidden gems in your community and help local businesses thrive.

🎯 PERFECT FOR:

• Budget-conscious foodies
• Happy hour enthusiasts
• Lunch deal hunters
• Date night planners
• Food explorers looking to try new places
• Anyone who loves great food at great prices

🌟 HOW IT WORKS:

1. Allow location access to see deals near you
2. Browse specials and coupons from local restaurants
3. Save your favorites or redeem coupons instantly
4. Get notifications about new deals at your favorite spots
5. Share great finds with friends and family

Download On the Cheap today and start saving on delicious meals!
