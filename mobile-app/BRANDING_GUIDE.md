# On-the-Cheap App - Branding Guide

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Brand Orange**: #f97316 (rgb(249, 115, 22))
  - Use for: Main UI elements, buttons, highlights
  - Represents: Energy, appetite, affordability

- **Dark Orange**: #ea580c (rgb(234, 88, 12))
  - Use for: Hover states, emphasis
  
- **Light Orange**: #ffedd5 (rgb(255, 237, 213))
  - Use for: Backgrounds, subtle accents

### Secondary Colors
- **White**: #ffffff - Clean backgrounds
- **Dark Gray**: #1f2937 - Text
- **Light Gray**: #f3f4f6 - Secondary backgrounds
- **Green**: #10b981 - Success states (deals active)
- **Red**: #ef4444 - Urgent/expiring deals

## Logo/Icon Concepts

### Option 1: Percent Symbol Icon
- Large % symbol in orange
- Circle background
- Clean, minimal design
- Clearly represents "deals/discounts"

### Option 2: Fork & Knife Icon
- Crossed fork and knife in orange
- Represents restaurant/dining
- With small discount badge or % symbol

### Option 3: "OTC" Monogram
- Letters O-T-C in creative arrangement
- Modern, geometric style
- Orange color scheme

### Option 4: Combined Icon (RECOMMENDED)
- Restaurant/food icon (fork, knife, or plate)
- With integrated % or coupon symbol
- Orange gradient background
- Clean, recognizable at small sizes

---

## App Icon Design Specifications

### Requirements
- **Size**: 1024x1024px (base size)
- **Format**: PNG with transparency OR solid background
- **Safe Zone**: Keep important elements within 90% center
- **Corners**: Don't add rounded corners (handled by OS)
- **Text**: Avoid small text (icon is viewed at 40-180px)
- **Complexity**: Simple is better - recognizable at thumbnail size

### Platform-Specific
- **iOS**: Auto-applies rounded corners and shadow
- **Android**: Uses adaptive icon (foreground + background)

## Brand Voice & Messaging

### Tone
- Friendly and approachable
- Value-focused ("save money", "best deals")
- Local and community-oriented
- Enthusiastic but not pushy

### Key Messages
- "Save money dining out"
- "Support local restaurants"
- "Never miss a deal"
- "Dine smarter, not harder"

### Avoid
- "Cheap" food (use "affordable", "value", "deals")
- Overly promotional language
- Complicated jargon
