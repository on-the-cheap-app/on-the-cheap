EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

THE OPPORTUNITY

The food service and hospitality industry faces a persistent challenge: underutilized operational capacity, particularly during off-peak hours. Daily specials, happy hours, and time-sensitive discounts have proven effective at boosting sales, attracting new customers, and increasing visit frequency. Yet, most restaurants lack sophisticated tools to promote these offers in real-time to nearby, intent-driven consumers.

Simultaneously, consumers increasingly seek convenience, value, and informed decision-making when dining out. They want real-time access to deals, ratings, and direct engagement with restaurants—without the friction of third-party aggregators.

THE SOLUTION

On-the-Cheap is a two-sided platform connecting local food service businesses with value-conscious consumers through:

For Consumers: A free mobile app and web platform offering real-time, hyper-local restaurant deals, specials, and happy hours with GPS-enabled discovery

For Restaurant Owners: A SaaS platform with tiered subscriptions providing promotional tools, customer analytics, and AI-powered business intelligence

KEY DIFFERENTIATORS

1. Holistic Value Proposition: Single-source solution for all deal types (daily specials, happy hours, events) across all meal periods

2. Data-Driven Partnership: Actionable analytics transform the relationship from transactional to strategic

3. AI-Powered Features: Dynamic pricing optimization and intelligent upselling (Enterprise tier)

4. Direct Customer Relationships: Restaurants retain customer data, unlike third-party aggregators

CURRENT STATUS

• Web App: Live at www.onthecheapapp.com
• Android App: In Google Play closed testing (production release Feb 2026)
• iOS App: Planned Q1 2026
• Payments: Stripe integration live and functional
